About Dr. Cris

Whether you are struggling with a particular issue, or just can't shake the feeling that there should be more to life, Dr. Cris Abad-Santos can help. Her practice is based on the belief that her clients' needs are of the utmost importance.

Dr. Cris Abad-Santos provides a caring environment in which you can identify and explore those issues that may be getting in the way of a more rewarding home life and career, or of more fulfilling relationships. The goal is to help provide you with insights and tools you need to move beyond old, limiting beliefs and behaviors.

Dr. Cris Abad-Santos is aware that many psychiatric conditions are accompanied by social discomfort and embarrassment, therefore she emphasizes sensitivity and discretion when dealing with her clients. She would welcome the opportunity to earn your trust and deliver you the best services she could offer and provide.
