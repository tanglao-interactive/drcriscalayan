Unbroken

My Journey as a Foreign Medical Graduate in the U.S.

Unbroken follows Dr. Cris Calayan Abad Santos through the emotional and professional terrain of becoming a doctor across countries, starting over in the United States, and raising a family through uncertainty.

It is a story about reinvention, survival, faith, and the unbreakable human spirit.
