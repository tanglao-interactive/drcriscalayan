The Author

Dr. Cris Calayan Abad Santos

Dr. Cris Calayan Abad Santos is a physician, author, entrepreneur, immigrant, and mother whose story spans continents, careers, and personal reinvention.

After graduating from medical school in the Philippines, she pursued her dream of practicing medicine in the United States while raising her children and navigating the challenges of building a new life in a foreign country.

Her memoir is written for foreign medical graduates, immigrant parents, and anyone who has had to rebuild from the beginning without letting adversity define them.
