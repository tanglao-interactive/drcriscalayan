Our Office

A Professional Dedicated To Listening

Whether you are struggling with a particular issue, or just can't shake the feeling that there should be more to life, Dr. Abad-Santos can help. She provides a caring environment in which you can identify and explore those issues that may be getting in the way of a more rewarding home life and career, or of more fulfilling relationships. The goal is to help provide you with the insights and tools you need to move beyond old, limiting beliefs and behaviors.

A combination of medication management and psychotherapeutic techniques will help you identify limiting beliefs and outmoded coping mechanisms that may be standing in your way. Many clients report rapid progress due to effective approaches that are employed. All communications with your psychiatrist are kept strictly confidential.

Some of the common psychiatric conditions that a psychiatrist will diagnose and treat are major depressive disorder, bipolar disorder, attention deficit hyperactivity disorder, obsessive-compulsive disorder, , generalized anxiety disorder, postpartum , eating disorder, post-traumatic stress disorder, drug addictions, alcoholism, relationship problems, self-esteem issues, adjustment disorder, schizophrenia and schizoaffective disorders.

Dr. Abad-Santos also serves as a consultant to other physicians as well as provide long-term care to clients with chronic psychiatric conditions.

Dr. Abad-Santos is focused on providing high-quality service and customer satisfaction. She will do everything she can to meet your expectations.
